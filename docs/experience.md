## National Cyber League

The NCL is a collegiate CTF with an **Individual Game** and a **Team Game**, scored
across eight domains. My documented work is strongest in **OSINT** and **Log Analysis**,
with solid coverage in **Password Cracking**, **Cryptography**, and **Network Traffic
Analysis**. See [Skills & Tooling](skills.md) for the techniques and tools, and
[Write-ups](writeups/index.md) for sanitized solves.

## Liber8tion CTF

A separate, password-cracking–focused event. It pushed a range of hash-attack workflows:
rebuilding passwords from substrings via combinator chaining, recovering an encrypted
vault's key, cracking `sha256crypt` shadow hashes, and — the standout — cracking
**non-ASCII (Unicode) passwords by attacking their UTF-8 byte sequences**. The reusable
tooling and methodology from this work live in [Tools](tools.md).
