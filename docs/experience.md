## Cyber Skyline — Fall 2026

I competed in the **Cyber Skyline Fall 2026 CTF** as part of my SANS.edu program — my
first time applying the techniques I'd been studying in a live event. I didn't go in
chasing accuracy or placement; I went in **to learn** — to build retention, and to find
out where I could locate the information I needed under pressure. My SANS notes carried
me through what was, at first, an intimidating experience.

As **team captain** for the Team Game, I was fortunate to have a communicative, dedicated
team that made leading genuinely enjoyable. I'm grateful to everyone who was part of it.
It was a valuable first step, and I'm looking forward to the challenges ahead.

My documented work spans eight domains, and is strongest in **OSINT** and **Log
Analysis**, with solid coverage in **Password Cracking**, **Cryptography**, and **Network
Traffic Analysis**. See [Skills & Tooling](skills.md) for techniques and tools, and
[Write-ups](writeups/index.md) for sanitized solves.
