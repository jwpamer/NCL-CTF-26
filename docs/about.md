# About / Background

## My path to cybersecurity

My path into cybersecurity didn't start in a classroom. After serving in the **United
States Marine Corps**, I set out to earn a degree in Economics at Penn State University.
It didn't take long to realize Economics wasn't the work I saw myself doing day in and
day out, so I took time to reflect on what genuinely interested me.

I kept coming back to the hours I spent around my father — surrounded by electronics,
watching him clear viruses from people's computers and build machines from parts — and
my uncle, who I'd often find running Linux and exploring systems. That's where this
journey really began. I transitioned from Economics to Cybersecurity, drawn by their
influence and by a field whose importance keeps growing.

Partway through, I realized the program I was in wasn't the right fit for how I wanted
to learn. I started asking questions, watching talks, and researching where I could get
the most out of my education — and that search led me to **SANS.edu**, where I am today.
